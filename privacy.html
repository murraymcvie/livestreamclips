Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

What we collect from TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account, the app receives:

  • your basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID), shown on the posting screen so you can see which account you are posting to;
  • access and refresh tokens, so the app can upload the videos you choose to post.

We do not collect your followers, messages, viewing history or any other TikTok data.

Where it is stored

All of this stays on the operator's own computer. It is not sent to any server of ours, sold or shared, and it is not used for advertising.

Videos

Videos you choose to post are uploaded directly to TikTok through TikTok's Content Posting API, together with the caption and settings you chose.

Livestream data

To find highlights, the app reads public chat messages and audio from the livestreams of creators who have given permission. Chat is only used to measure reactions (for example, how much chat is laughing) and is not stored with usernames or shared.

Removing your data

Disconnect the app in TikTok (Settings, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions) and its tokens stop working immediately. The operator can also delete the stored tokens at any time. Creators who want a clip removed can use the contact below.
